## Welcome to Fans of LeFox

This project has the sole purpose of promoting the actress Jorja Fox, found at [JorjaFox.net](https://jorjafox.net).

Due to the nature of materials (images, news articles, transcripts etc) we can claim no copyright or ownership over much of the content. That said, we make use of GitHub to manage and organize the code, track changes, and ensure the consistent uptime and functionality of the website.

## Active Projects

* [Website](https://github.com/JorjaFox/website) - A [Hugo](https://gohugo.com) powered repository of all our information, plus a blog, based on [Hinode](https://gethinode.org).
* [Bootstrap5 Lightbox Mod for Hugo](https://github.com/JorjaFox/mod-bs5-lightbox) - Requires Bootstrap 5, and Hinode.

## Former Projects

* WordPress Plugin - This managed the [WordPress](https://wordpress.org) side of things, such as Gutenberg Blocks, Custom Post Types, Comment Management, and more.
* Library - A [Hugo](https://gohugo.com) powered repository of all of Jorja's media appearances, interviews, and news articles (moved to new website)
* Gallery - A [NetPhotoGraphics](https://github.com/netPhotoGraphics/netPhotoGraphics/) powered gallery.
* Embed NPG - Embed NetPhotoGraphics albums and photos.
